States log · Instagram Story maker

Make your travel story

Answer a few quick questions, tap the states or countries you've been to, and download a ready-to-post Instagram Story (1080 × 1920). No coding needed.

1Where are you now?

Who you are today and the role you're aiming for. The sample answers are placeholders, so replace them with your own.

2Your skills

Rate each skill in words, not percentages. Groups become the branches and practice areas; up to 4 groups look best.

3Certificates

Name, who issued it, and the year. Leave empty if you have none yet.

4Projects

One measurable result per project. Tags are skills, separated by commas. Don't post confidential employer numbers.

5Milestones

Dates as year and month, like 2026-05. They become the timeline and activity grid.

6Choose a design and size

Story is 1080 × 1920 for Instagram. LinkedIn is 1080 × 1350, the 4:5 feed size, so nothing gets cropped. The LinkedIn size leaves out some sections to keep it readable.
